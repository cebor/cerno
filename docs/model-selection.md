## Verdict

**`gemma4:e2b-it-qat`** — the most accurate model that answered every case with a usable label, ties broken by median latency.

Set it as the default with `CERNO_DEFAULT_MODEL=gemma4:e2b-it-qat`. Its best-fit calibration temperature above is 0.80; a value below 1 means the model is slightly *under*confident and would need sharpening rather than flattening. cerno ships a default of 1.0 either way — 36 labelled cases is thin evidence for baking in an adjustment, and callers can always pass their own.

## Reading the table

- **Fidelity** — share of cases answered with one of the offered letters. This is a gate,
  not a score: below 100% the model is ignoring the instruction on some inputs, and no
  amount of accuracy makes up for an answer cerno cannot read.
- **Accuracy** — share of cases where the picked label was the labelled one. Score cases
  allow the per-case tolerance in the dataset.
- **p50 / p99** — wall-clock per question, warm model, one question per request.
- **Truncated** — share of answers where some label fell outside the host's top-20 window,
  making its probability an upper bound rather than an observation.
- **Brier** — mean squared error of the yes-probability on noul cases; lower is better.
- **Best T** — the calibration temperature that best explains the labelled outcomes. Far
  above 1 means the model is overconfident and needs flattening.
- **Agreement** — share of cases where the model picked the same label as the reference.
